Using gap analysis to shortlist an ERP system

ERP · Requirements

A gap analysis compares what the business needs with what a system does out of the box. It is the fastest way to rule vendors in or out before a costly demo cycle.

Start from processes, not features

List your ten most important processes, such as purchase-to-pay or order-to-cash. For each, write the steps a user takes today and the output the business needs.

Score each gap

ScoreMeaning
0Met out of the box
1Met with configuration
2Needs custom development
3Not met, and a workaround is needed

Compare vendors on the same scripts

Give every vendor the same scenarios, with the same data, and ask them to demonstrate them. Vendors who only show their strengths will be obvious in the scores.

Report the cost of gaps

A score of 2 with a heavy custom build may cost more than a licence difference. Present the gap cost in money and time so the steering committee can compare options fairly.
