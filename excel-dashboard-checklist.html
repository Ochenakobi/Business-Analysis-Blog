A checklist for building Excel dashboards people use

Excel · Reporting

Most dashboards fail because nobody agreed on the questions they answer. Settle those first, then build.

Before you open Excel

  • Write the three decisions this dashboard supports, and who makes them.
  • List the metrics, with one definition each. "Sales" is not a definition.
  • Confirm the source system, refresh frequency and who owns the data.

While you build

  • Keep raw data on its own sheet, untouched. Load it with Power Query where possible.
  • Use one calculation sheet with named ranges, so formulas read in plain words.
  • Put filters in one place at the top, and label units on every chart.
  • Avoid merged cells in any range a formula or pivot needs.

Before handover

  • Reconcile three totals against the source system by hand.
  • Test with a blank filter, a filter that returns no rows, and the largest period in the data.
  • Write a one-page guide: what each sheet is for, how to refresh, and who to ask.

After handover

Ask the first users to explain the dashboard back to you. Where they get it wrong, the design is wrong, not the users. Fix the labels before adding features.
