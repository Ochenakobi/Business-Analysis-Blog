Stakeholder mapping for business analysts

Stakeholder management · Business analysis

Most project delays come from people who were not asked early enough. A simple map shows who to involve, how much, and when.

List every group affected

Include users, approvers, system owners, finance, and anyone who will lose a task when the new process goes live. Missing a group usually means a missed requirement.

Place each person on an influence and interest grid

People with high influence and high interest need regular working sessions. People with high influence and low interest need short updates before decisions. Those with high interest and low influence need clear channels to raise concerns.

Record what each person needs to hear

A factory manager cares about production downtime. A finance controller cares about month-end timing. Write one line per stakeholder on what matters to them, and check it before each steering meeting.

Update the map monthly

Roles change, and new approvers appear during a project. A map that was accurate in month one can mislead you by month six.
